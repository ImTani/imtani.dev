# Copyright and licensing

This repository contains two kinds of work under two different terms, and the
split is deliberate.

configuration is licensed under the GPL-3.0. The full text is in `LICENSE`.

You may use, modify and redistribute it, including the simulation engine, on
the condition that derivative works carry the same licence.

## Everything else — all rights reserved

Not covered by the GPL, and not licensed for reuse:

- **Written content** — prose, narrator lines, headings, section copy
- **Visual design** — the visual language, layout, colour, typography choices,
  logotype and any marks
- **Artwork** — drawings, illustration, and the creature's design
- **Audio** — music, ambience and interface sound
- **The name** *Tanishk Narula*, the domain *imtani.dev*, and the identities
  *ImTani* and *infiniTani*

## Why it is split this way

The site makes a specific claim about its own authorship: **the code here is
written with AI. The art is not.** A single permissive licence over the whole
repository would quietly contradict that — it would put the hand-made half on
the same footing as the generated half and invite exactly the reuse the claim
exists to refuse.

So the machine-assisted part is free, copyleft, and yours to build on. The part
that came out of a person's head is not.
